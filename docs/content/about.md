## About me {#about}

I teach and do research in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Information Systems, and User Experience (UX).

- **Education:** PhD in Information Science, with a specialization in Human-Computer Interaction, University at Albany. MBA in Marketing & Information Systems.
- **Dissertation:** "Sticky Words: A Computational Linguistics Approach", which received the Distinguished Dissertation and Excellence in Research Awards.
- **Research interests:** User Experience and Human-Computer Interaction, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Computational Linguistics and Natural Language Processing, Behavioral Economics, Information Systems Education.
- **Teaching:** courses in information technology, business analytics, Python programming, digital product design, and web design. A few of them:
    - BITM 330 - Improving Business Performance with Information Technologies
    - BITM 522 - Managing Information Technology
    - ISBA 434 - Business Analytics Practicum
    - INF 108 - Programming for Problem Solving: Introduction to Python
